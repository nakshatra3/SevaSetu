1. User Registration & Login — Volunteer, Organization, and Admin roles.
2. Volunteer Opportunities — Organizations/individuals can post activities such as teaching, beach cleaning, tree plantation, food distribution, etc.
3. Location-Based Discovery — Find volunteering opportunities within a selected radius using maps/location.
4. Search & Filters — Filter by activity type, distance, date, duration, skills, and availability.
5. Event Registration — Volunteers can join, cancel, and view upcoming activities.
6. Event Management — Organizers can manage volunteers, capacity, schedules, and event details.
7. Volunteer Profiles — Skills, interests, volunteering history, total hours, completed activities, and achievements.
8. Attendance/Check-in — QR-based or organizer-verified attendance to confirm participation.
9. Gamification — Points for volunteering, donations, event participation, and helping recruit volunteers.
10. Leaderboards — Global, city, college, monthly, and organization-wise rankings.
11. Badges & Achievements — Awards such as Green Warrior, Community Hero, and 100-Hour Volunteer.
12. Donation Tracking — Record donations such as books, clothes, food, or money.
13. Organization Profiles — Organization information, activities, events, volunteers, and ratings.
14. Ratings & Reviews — Volunteers can review organizations/events and organizers can provide participation feedback.
15. Notifications — Event reminders, new nearby opportunities, registration updates, and achievements.
16. Verification & Reporting — Verified organizations, report users/events, and basic safety features.
17. Admin Dashboard — Manage users, organizations, events, reports, categories, badges, and platform statistics.
18. Smart Volunteer Matching — Recommend opportunities based on location + interests + skills + availability + previous activities.
